two world records

Essentials of Hematology: Inside Hematology

In 2018, he authored "Essentials of Hematology: Inside Hematology," published in English, Hindi, and Russian. It was acknowledged as a world record for the "Youngest person to write a trilingual book."

2019

The Historical Development of the Heart i.e., from its Formation from...

In 2019, he authored "The Historical Development of the Heart i.e., from its Formation from...", and received the Guinness World Record for the "longest title of a book" with 3,777 words and 26,021 characters.
