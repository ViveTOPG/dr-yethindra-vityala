2020Fastest Research Study Accomplished in the WorldIn 2020, Dr. Yethindra Vityala set a world record for the fastest research study, marking a milestone in his medical-science career. Despite the rapid pace, the study met stringent standards, demonstrating that swift execution and academic integrity can coexist and enhance each other when managed effectively.High Range Book of World Records
2019First person in the world to complete 20 medical courses at 10 universities in 9 daysIn 2019, Dr. Yethindra Vityala completed 20 medical courses at 10 universities in 9 days. This challenging endeavor requires intense focus and understanding to meet formal academic standards.World Record Certification Agency
2019Youngest person to write a trilingual bookIn 2019, Dr. Yethindra Vityala received a world record as the youngest person to write a trilingual book, "Essentials of Hematology", in three languages: English, Hindi, and Russian. Writing a medical book in three languages at a young age is an extraordinary achievement.World Record Certification Agency
2019Most Medical Related Certificates Received in 9 DaysIn 2019, Dr. Yethindra Vityala set a world record by obtaining 51 medical-related certificates in 9 days. These certificates were obtained from various universities, organizations, and educational platforms, reflecting consistent academic performance across disciplines in a short period.World Record Certification Agency
